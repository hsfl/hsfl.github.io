About HSFL

The Hawaiʻi Space Flight Laboratory was established in 2007 within the School of Ocean and Earth Science and Technology (SOEST) and the College of Engineering at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa.

HSFL develops 1–100 kg small satellites for science and education, supports technology validation missions for government agencies such as NASA and the DoD, and partners with university and industry missions. Students work side by side with engineers and scientists on every phase, from concept to on-orbit operations.

Satellite platforms

CubeSats and microsatellites, from bus design through flight.

Integration & testing

Cleanroom, thermal-vacuum and environmental test capability.

Instrumentation

Science payloads such as hyperspectral thermal imagers and neutron detectors.

Mission operations

Ground stations across Hawaiʻi and the COSMOS operations software.

Education & outreach

HSFL trains the next generation of Hawaiʻi's aerospace workforce. Undergraduate and graduate students join mission teams, run ground stations and write flight code, while the Artemis CubeSat Kit and partnerships with community colleges bring hands-on space engineering to classrooms statewide.

Interested in joining a mission team, collaborating on research, or contributing to our open-source software? Get in touch, or open an issue or pull request on any of our repositories.
